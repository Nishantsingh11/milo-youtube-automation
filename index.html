Milo YouTube Automation

Milo YouTube Automation uploads short cartoon videos for kids to the owner's own YouTube channels, on a schedule the owner set.

Channels

  • Milo the Cat - Kids Songs
  • Milo Counts - Kids Numbers
  • Milo Colours - Kids Colours
  • Milo Hindi Rhymes

How it works

  1. The owner makes a cartoon Short and approves its title and description.
  2. The owner authorises the app for one channel at a time, using Google sign-in, with only two permissions: upload videos, and view the account to confirm which channel is authorised.
  3. A scheduled run uploads the approved video to that channel, as Private until the YouTube audit passes, and reports the result to the owner.

What it does not do

  • It does not serve other users, collect other people's data, or share Google user data with any third party.
  • It does not read comments, analytics, or watch history.
